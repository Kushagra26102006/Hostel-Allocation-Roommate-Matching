# Warden & Administrator Operations Guide

This guide covers operational workflows for Hostel Administrators, Block Wardens, and Chief Wardens managing campus residential allocations.

---

## 1. Authentication & Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

All staff accounts (`warden`, `chief_warden`, `hostel_admin`, `sys_admin`) have mandatory MFA:

1. Log in at `/login` with staff credentials.
2. The system prompts for a 6-digit TOTP token generated by your authenticator application (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, etc.).
3. Store your single-use recovery backup codes in a secure institutional password manager.

---

## 2. Managing Inventory (`/staff/admin/inventory`)

1. **Inventory Hierarchy:** Hostels ──► Blocks ──► Floors ──► Rooms ──► Beds.
2. **Bulk CSV Import Wizard:**
   - Navigate to `/staff/admin/inventory` and click **Import CSV**.
   - Download the standard CSV template.
   - Upload your campus room inventory spreadsheet.
   - The wizard executes a dry-run validation checking room numbers, capacities, accessibility flags, and gender assignments before writing to the database.
3. **Bed Statuses:**
   - `free`: Available for algorithmic allocation.
   - `assigned`: Occupied by a student.
   - `held`: Reserved for guest faculty, emergencies, or quarantine.
   - `maintenance`: Blocked due to ongoing repairs or painting.

---

## 3. Configuring Allocation Cycles & Running Matching (`/staff/admin/cycles`)

1. **Cycle Setup:**
   - Define cycle name (e.g. "Monsoon 2026 UG Allocation").
   - Set application submission window open and close timestamps.
   - Configure quota capacity percentages ($SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PWD$).
2. **Executing the Allocation Algorithm:**
   - Once the application window closes, click **Run Allocation**.
   - The server triggers the background worker via BullMQ.
   - Watch live allocation progress via Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming real-time metrics, stage progress, and composite score distributions.
   - Upon completion, the system generates an unapproved **Allocation Draft**.

---

## 4. Warden Review Console & Manual Overrides (`/staff/warden/review/[id]`)

1. **Interactive Bed Map:**
   - Review room-by-room placements, floor occupancy heat maps, and compatibility scores.
   - Wardens can only review and override beds within their formally assigned residential blocks.
2. **Manual Overrides with Mandatory Reasons:**
   - To reassign a student, click the student's assigned bed and select **Manual Override**.
   - Choose the target destination bed.
   - Enter a **mandatory reason** (minimum 10 characters).
   - The system re-validates all hard constraints (gender, cohort, accessibility) in real time.
   - **Optimistic Concurrency Control:** If another warden edited the same draft in the interim, the system prevents silent overwrites with a `409 Version Conflict` alert, allowing you to review the latest state.
3. **Audit Trail Recording:**
   - Every manual override is hashed and anchored into the institutional SHA-256 audit chain.

---

## 5. Maker-Checker Approval Ceremony & Publication

To prevent unilateral, unauthorized publication, HostelHub enforces a strict **Four-Layer Maker-Checker Gate**:

1. **Step 1 (Warden Submission):** After reviewing overrides, the block warden clicks **Submit for Approval**. Draft transitions to `READY_FOR_APPROVAL`.
2. **Step 2 (Chief Warden Review):**
   - The Chief Warden inspects the **Draft Diff Summary** showing all additions, moves, and override reasons.
   - If any override was flagged as escalated (e.g. transferring an able-bodied resident into a certified accessible bed), a second distinct approver (Dean of Student Welfare) is required.
   - Chief Warden signs off with a recorded approval comment, creating an immutable `ApprovalRecord`.
3. **Step 3 (Provisional Publication):**
   - Click **Publish Allocation**.
   - Allocation letters are generated with Ed25519 digital signatures.
   - Notification dispatches are queued via Web Push and Email.
   - Results become visible to students on their portal.

---

## 6. Dynamic Waitlist Management & Bed Vacations (`/staff/warden/waitlist`)

When an allocated student withdraws, transfers, or fails to report during the move-in window:

1. Navigate to `/staff/warden/waitlist`.
2. Click **Mark Bed Vacated**.
3. Select the vacated bed and record the trigger reason (`withdrawal`, `no_show`, `appeal_granted`).
4. The system executes the **Dynamic Waitlist Evaluator**:
   - Matches the highest-ranked feasible waitlisted student conforming to the bed's gender, programme, and quota criteria.
   - Under `auto_confirm` policy, the top candidate is promoted immediately and notified.
   - Live room occupancy statistics reconcile in real time.
